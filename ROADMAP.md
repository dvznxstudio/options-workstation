# Product Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Mobile Roadmap MVP
- Live SPY spot and options-chain normalization
- OCC prior-day OI
- GEX, DEX, expected move, walls and transitions
- Scenario engine with forming/active/invalidated states
- Mobile interface and manual refresh
- Transparent reason codes

## Phase 2 — Alert Engine
- Background checks every 1–5 minutes
- Trigger, target, invalidation and regime-change alerts
- Web Push, email and optional SMS
- Cooldowns and duplicate-alert prevention
- Quiet hours and customizable sensitivity

## Phase 3 — Intraday Confirmation
- VWAP, opening range and 9/21 EMA
- Interval volume differential
- Wall and centroid migration
- Acceptance/rejection logic
- Setup-quality score separated from directional bias

## Phase 4 — Validation
- Historical snapshot database
- Outcome tracking after every trigger
- Performance by regime, weekday, expiration and time of day
- Calibration of confidence scores
- No probability labels until validated on out-of-sample data

## Phase 5 — Multi-Asset Workstation
- SPX, QQQ, IWM and selected liquid equities
- Cross-market confirmation from VIX, ES and breadth
- Scanner and watchlists
- Trading journal and post-trade review
